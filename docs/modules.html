      Interfaces

      ConfigFile

      The config file: YAML, read at startup. An unknown key fails startup.

      ConfigApp

      An app in ConfigFile.apps.

      ConfigWebhook

      A webhook source of a ConfigApp.

      ConfigPlay

      The Google Play source of a ConfigApp.

      ConfigAscApi

      An App Store Connect API team key, from App Store Connect › Users and Access › Integrations › Team keys. The Developer role is enough.

      ConfigPlayApi

      A Google Cloud service account with read-only access to the apps in Play Console.

      ConfigIngest

      Settings for POST /ingest. The body is an IngestEvent.

      ConfigRoute

      Sends the events that match it to targets. Every matching route fires. A target matched by several routes gets one message, with a mention if any of them asks and silent only if all do.

      IngestEvent

      The JSON body of a POST /ingest, signed with Standard Webhooks. A missing or bad signature, or a timestamp more than 5 minutes off, gets a 401. An invalid body gets a 400 with the reason. Unknown keys are ignored.

      DiscordTarget

      A target in ConfigFile.targets that posts to a Discord webhook. Its name and avatar are set on the webhook in Discord.

      SlackTarget

      A target in ConfigFile.targets that posts to a Slack incoming webhook. The bot's name and icon come from the Slack app.

      TeamsTarget

      A target in ConfigFile.targets that posts to a Teams channel through a Workflows webhook.
